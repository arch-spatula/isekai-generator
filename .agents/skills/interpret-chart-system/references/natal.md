Cover the zodiac, position, node, time conversion, actual house system, and boundary rules. Cover every printed angle and house cusp; every printed planet, node, derived point, sign, degree, retrograde marker, and house; and every printed major aspect with its type and orb. Relate planets, signs, houses, angles, and aspect patterns without losing individual evidence.

Use these exact `해상도:` labels and cover each at least once: `심리 기능`, `삶의 영역`, `내적 역동`. Use configurations and repeated patterns as relational structures rather than listing placements independently.

Do not invent unprinted DESC, IC, points, houses, or aspects. If time-dependent placements are unavailable, derive `삶의 영역` only from the printed evidence.

Conditional absence markers: `Angles`, `Houses`, `Chiron`, `Fortuna`.
