# Interpretation document contract

Write the document in Korean with these headings exactly once and in this order:

1. `# 통합 차트 해석`
2. `## 해석 원칙`
3. `## 전문 해석 요약`
   - `### 사주팔자`
   - `### 자미두수`
   - `### 서양 점성술`
4. `## 교차 체계 비교`
   - `### 일관된 해석`
   - `### 구체성을 더하는 해석`
   - `### 상충하는 해석`
5. `## 핵심 해석 항목`

Under `## 핵심 해석 항목`, write items headed `### I-01`, `### I-02`, and so on with unique sequential IDs. Every item must contain `영역:`, `관계: 일관|구체화|상충`, `해석:`, and `근거:` lines and may contain `불확실성:`. Every evidence line must cite existing `S-`, `Z-`, or `N-` specialist IDs.

Cover each exact domain label at least once: `성격과 기질`, `직업과 적성`, `재물`, `연애와 배우자`, `가족과 대인관계`, `건강과 생활 리듬`, `시기운`. Retain the specialists' concrete life reading; do not reduce it to statements that systems merely agree or that an axis is emphasized.

Keep specialist summaries distinct from synthesis. Do not extract creative motifs or include complete specialist documents or any GDD design.
End after the interpretation items. Do not append a disclaimer or a general limitations section; record evidence-specific ambiguity only in the relevant item's optional `불확실성:` line.
