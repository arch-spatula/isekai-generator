# Interpretation document contract

Write the document in Korean with these headings exactly once and in this order:

1. `# 통합 차트 해석`
2. `## 해석 원칙`
3. `## 전문 해석 요약`
   - `### 사주팔자`
   - `### 자미두수`
   - `### 서양 점성술`
4. `## 교차 체계 비교`
   - `### 일관된 해석`
   - `### 구체성을 더하는 해석`
   - `### 상충하는 해석`
5. `## 통합 인물 구조`
   - `### 공통 핵심축`
   - `### 체계별 고유 통찰`
   - `### 영역 간 작동 구조`
   - `### 시간에 따른 변화`
6. `## 핵심 해석 항목`

Under `### 공통 핵심축`, write substantive `핵심 욕구:`, `반복 갈등:`, `의사결정 기준:`, and `근거:` lines. Cite at least two systems.

Under `### 체계별 고유 통찰`, write one concise line for each system in this form: `- 사주 고유: ... (근거: S-...)`, `- 자미두수 고유: ... (근거: Z-...)`, and `- 서양 점성술 고유: ... (근거: N-...)`. Explain what that system alone adds to the whole-person reading; do not restate a generic trait or cite another system on that line.

Under `### 영역 간 작동 구조`, write substantive `내적 동기:`, `행동 방식:`, `환경 반응:`, `반복 결과:`, and `근거:` lines. Connect multiple life domains and cite at least two systems. Under `### 시간에 따른 변화`, distinguish stable and changing expressions with `유지:`, `변화:`, and `근거:` lines, using only timing layers present in the specialists and citing at least two systems.

Under `## 핵심 해석 항목`, write items headed `### I-01`, `### I-02`, and so on with unique sequential IDs. Every item must contain `영역:`, a different `연결 영역:`, `관계: 일관|구체화|상충|고유`, `해석:`, `작동 사슬:`, `양면성: 강점: ... | 취약점: ...`, `조건:`, and `근거:` lines and may contain `불확실성:`. Write the operation chain as at least four evidence-supported stages separated by `→`. Cite existing IDs from at least two specialist systems for cross-system relationships. For `고유`, cite only IDs from the originating system.

Cover each exact domain label at least once: `성격과 기질`, `직업과 적성`, `재물`, `연애와 배우자`, `가족과 대인관계`, `건강과 생활 리듬`, `시기운`. Retain the specialists' concrete life reading; do not reduce it to statements that systems merely agree or that an axis is emphasized.

Keep specialist summaries distinct from synthesis. Treat common readings as the person's core axis and use system-specific readings to explain its cause, life arena, expression, timing, or exception. Do not flatten unique evidence into forced agreement. Do not extract creative motifs or include complete specialist documents or any GDD design.
End after the interpretation items. Do not append a disclaimer or a general limitations section; record evidence-specific ambiguity only in the relevant item's optional `불확실성:` line.
